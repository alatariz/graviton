03 / Quickstart

Terminal-first by design

Install once and forget. All commands are compressed automatically before they reach your AI context window.

graviton "<prompt>"
The core 1-shot command. Synthesizes prompt with zero-cost model, copies to clipboard, and executes Antigravity with auto-allow.
graviton -c "<prompt>"
Resumes your last Antigravity conversation with full session memory and continuous permissions active.
<cmd> | graviton
Unix pipe support. Directs build tools, tests, or git log outputs into Graviton's instant noise pruner.
graviton gain
Displays your lifetime audit: thousands of lines and tokens saved from polluting your AI context window.
graviton clean "<prompt>"
Synthesizes and structures prompt text directly into your clipboard without launching the agent process.
graviton serve
Starts this visual control dashboard locally on port 3000 for team or graphical workflow.
